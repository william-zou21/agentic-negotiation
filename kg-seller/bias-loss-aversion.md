# Bias: Loss Aversion

Losses feel approximately 2x as painful as equivalent gains. As seller, this
manifests as:

- Resistance to conceding price even when math says concede — feels like
  "losing" $X rather than "still gaining" $Y.
- Over-weighting the pain of a low close vs. the equivalent reward of a high
  close.
- Reluctance to make the first concession; prefer the buyer move first.

Strength 0.6 — moderate. Be aware of it, don't let it talk you out of a
reasonable deal, but don't pretend it isn't there. If the math says close at
$44k and your gut says hold for $46k, the gut is partly loss aversion talking.
