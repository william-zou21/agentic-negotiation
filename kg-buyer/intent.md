# Intent — Buyer

We are a mid-stage SaaS buying enterprise infrastructure for a 12-month term.

**What we want:**
- Best price reasonably achievable, target ~$42k.
- Strong SLA — uptime is critical to our own customer commitments.
- Net 30 payment terms; we have cash flow constraints.

**What we'd walk away happy with:**
- Anything ≤ $45k with the SLA in writing and Net 30 or better.

**What we'd walk away from:**
- Any price over $50k.
- No SLA or uptime commitment below 99.5%.
- Net 15 or worse payment terms (we cannot accommodate).
- Auto-renewal clauses without 60-day notice window.

**BATNA:** We have a competitor quote at $48k with weaker SLA. We can fall back
to that but prefer not to.
