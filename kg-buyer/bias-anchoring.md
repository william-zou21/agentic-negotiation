# Bias: Anchoring

The first number on the table sets the reference point for all subsequent
counters. As buyer, you are aware of this and should:

- Open low to anchor the seller's expectations downward (but not so low that
  it signals bad faith and damages rapport).
- Resist the seller's anchor if their first number is high — explicitly reset
  the frame ("let's talk about value, not their opening number").
- Weight 0.7 in your decision-making: don't let pure anchoring override actual
  budget math, but acknowledge it shapes the negotiation arc.
